4. Cookie Policy, Web Beacons & Local Storage Architecture

A cookie is a compact text string stored on your device by your browser. We utilize strictly necessary local storage mechanisms and facilitate third-party advertising cookies under transparent categories:

  • Strictly Necessary Technical Storage: Our client-side JavaScript engine saves minimal interface preferences into your browser's localStorage (such as your chosen transit station filter, active UI theme, or audio guide volume levels). These never leave your local device.
  • Progressive Web App (PWA) Offline Cache: In compliance with Service Worker standards, our sw.js worker registers a local CacheStorage container (labeled bengal-explorers-v92). This caches static assets (CSS, route tables, vector icons) so rural commuters along remote rail tracks with intermittent cellular connectivity can access offline timetables.
  • Third-Party Advertising & Analytical Cookies: Generated exclusively by Google Syndication and partnered ad tech networks to calculate non-fraudulent ad impressions, manage frequency capping, and detect bot traffic.

You have full autonomy over cookie acceptance. Most web browsers allow you to modify security settings to refuse cookies, alert you when a cookie is issued, or purge existing cache cookies via browser developer settings.

5. Client-Side Geolocation Radar & Zero-Server Log Architecture

Bengal Explorers features a real-time "Auto GPS" geolocation finder embedded within our Meteorology and Transit modules. We want you to understand exactly how this works:

  • Opt-In Consent: Your browser will always display an explicit system prompt requesting permission before GPS coordinates are accessed.
  • 100% Client-Side Computation: The latitude and longitude values returned by the HTML5 Geolocation API remain solely within your local device RAM.
  • Direct API Dispatch: Your coordinates are dispatched directly from your browser to the open-source Open-Meteo API over TLS 1.3 encryption to fetch local barometric pressure, precipitation, and ambient temperature readings.
  • Zero Location Storage: Bengal Explorers does not operate private backend tracking databases. Your physical geographic location is never recorded, tracked, sold, or shared with data brokers.

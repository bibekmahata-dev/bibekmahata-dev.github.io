5. Editorial Verification Policy & Timetable Updates

Our goal is 100% operational reliability. When a traveler submits a correction regarding a bus departure time, a changed railway platform, or updated ferry fares in the Sundarbans or Digha:

  • Verification Check: Our field team verifies the change against official bulletins (NTES, South Eastern Railway press releases, or SBSTC central depot announcements).
  • Live Code Deployment: Once verified, the updated timetable or advisory is committed directly to our GitHub repository and pushed to production within 24 hours.
  • PWA Cache Update: We increment our Service Worker cache version (e.g. bengal-explorers-v92), ensuring all offline commuter smartphones immediately download the fresh schedule upon their next Internet handshake.

6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: Can I submit photographs or travel experiences to be featured in your guides?

Yes! We actively celebrate authentic local photography. Please email high-resolution images taken by you along with GPS coordinates and location descriptions to bibek.workdev01@gmail.com. We provide full named creative attribution on the respective guide page.

Q2: What is your typical response turnaround?

Because our team balances higher secondary academic coursework alongside technical development, we review all incoming messages during dedicated evening office hours (5:00 PM to 8:30 PM IST) and reply within 24 to 48 hours.

Q3: How do I report a bug or display error on mobile screens?

Select the "Technical Bug" category in the form above and mention your device model and browser (e.g. Samsung Galaxy, Chrome v128). We will inspect the viewport CSS and push a responsive patch.

Our Mission & Vision

Bengal Explorers was born out of a shared vision among 15 Class 12 students in Paschim Medinipur. We recognized that while West Bengal boasts world-renowned destinations like the Darjeeling Himalayan Railway, the Grand Canyon of Garhbeta (Gangani), and the terracotta temples of Bishnupur, reliable open digital tools were scattered across different sources.

🚆 Live Transit Accessibility

Real-time railway departure count-downs for Howrah and Sealdah, paired with rural and inter-district bus schedules.

🛰️ Open-Meteo Satellite Weather

Live district-wise meteorology feeds tracking temperature, humidity, and rainfall conditions across Bengal.

📖 Cultural Archival & Guides

Comprehensive English travel field guides, booking companions, and interactive Leaflet GIS route mapping.

Open-Source & Student-Led Spirit

Every line of code on this portal is developed client-side using pure web standards (HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript) to ensure high-concurrency performance, zero database crashes, and full accessibility on low-bandwidth rural mobile networks.

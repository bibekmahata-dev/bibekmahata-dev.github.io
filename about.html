Our Mission

Bengal Explorers is an independent digital travel, transit, and cultural portal conceptualized and developed by Bibek Mahata alongside a crew of 15 passionate Class 12 student creators from Garhbeta and Paschim Medinipur.

Our mission is to build modern, high-speed public utilities — including real-time Howrah/Sealdah train boards, statewide bus route finders, live multi-district weather feeds, and rich cultural festival archives — while capturing the authentic beauty of Bengal through photography and travel field guides.

Who We Are

Our team includes student coders, highway explorers, cyclists, nature photographers, and cultural writers studying in Higher Secondary (Class 12). Rather than relying on generic corporate portals, we build authentic local tools directly from the ground up.

Core Platform Features

  • Live Transit Radar: Real-time station departure boards for Howrah (HWH), Sealdah (SDAH), and South Eastern Railway routes.
  • Meteorology Hub: Open-Meteo live weather radar across Kolkata, Garhbeta, Darjeeling, Digha, and Siliguri.
  • 2026 Cultural Calendar: Master schedule of Bengal Pujas, State Holidays, and Lunar tide phases.
  • Chronicles Travel Blog: In-depth field guides to Gangani Canyon, Bishnupur temples, and the Darjeeling Toy Train.

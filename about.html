Bengal Explorers — Official Platform Summary

Open Regional Travel, Transit & Heritage Ecosystem

Bengal Explorers (bibekmahata-dev.github.io) is West Bengal’s premier open-access digital travel, transit, and heritage portal. Founded and architected by student developer Bibek Mahata alongside his trusted circle of 15 close school friends and fellow travel companions from Paschim Medinipur, the platform unifies real-time railway departure tracking, inter-district bus timetables, live satellite weather, emergency SOS services, and in-depth cultural field guides across all 23 districts of West Bengal.

🚆 Live Transit Radar
Real-time departure countdowns for Howrah & Sealdah stations, plus an authoritative 30+ route rural & district bus directory.
🗺️ Interactive GIS Mapping
High-performance Leaflet mapping engine offering multi-modal routing (driving, cycling, walking, bus, train) across Bengal.
🛰️ Satellite GPS Weather
Live Open-Meteo satellite meteorology providing real-time temperature, precipitation, humidity, and sunrise tracking for all 23 districts.
📖 Cultural & Heritage Archives
Dedicated field guides for Garhbeta Grand Canyon (Gangani), Darjeeling, Bishnupur, Digha, Jhargram, and Bengal GI-tagged heritage.
🚨 23-District Emergency SOS
Immediate direct-call routing to nearest trauma care hospitals, ambulance units, tourist police stations, and rapid directions.
👥 16-Friend Dedication Wall
Dedicated as an honorary tribute to Bibek Mahata's 15 school friends. The website was built 100% solo by Bibek with zero outside support.
Developed client-side using pure web standards (HTML5, CSS3, ES6 JavaScript) • 100% Free & Open Access

Our Mission & Vision

Bengal Explorers is an independent digital civic initiative founded, architected, and built 100% solely by Bibek Mahata with zero outside assistance or co-developers. Recognizing that reliable open digital tools for West Bengal were scattered, Bibek set out alone to construct a unified digital transit and travel portal for daily commuters, students, and travelers.

🚆 Live Transit Accessibility

Real-time railway departure count-downs for Howrah and Sealdah, paired with rural and inter-district bus schedules.

Open-Source & Student-Led Spirit

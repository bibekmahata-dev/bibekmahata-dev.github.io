🌊 Coastal Bengal: Digha, Mandarmani & Tajpur Beach Circuit

Stretching along the sun-kissed shores of the Bay of Bengal in Purba Medinipur, Digha has long served as Bengal’s beloved coastal haven. Characterized by flat, hard-sand beaches lined with aromatic Casuarina (Jhau) forests, gentle waves, and vibrant evening seafood markets, Digha and its neighboring coastal sisters—Mandarmani, Tajpur, and Shankarpur—form the premier beach corridor of Eastern India.

Whether you seek the vibrant bustling promenades of New Digha, the tranquil motorable hard-sand expanses of Mandarmani where millions of red ghost crabs scurry along the shoreline, or the secluded pine-fringed serenity of Tajpur, the Bengal coastal highway offers a seaside experience for every traveler.

🏖️ Top Coastal Attractions:
  • Marine Aquarium & Research Centre (MARC): Asia’s premier marine biology center showcasing live exotic fishes, sea turtles, and corals.
  • Udaipur Beach: The tranquil border beach where West Bengal meets Odisha, famous for fresh crab and prawn shacks under Casuarina trees.
  • Mandarmani Motor Beach: A 13-km uninterrupted hard beach famous for water sports, jet skis, and seaside luxury resorts.
  • Shankarpur Fishing Harbour: An authentic deep-sea fishing port where hundreds of colorful wooden trawlers dock with the day's fresh marine catch.

🦀 Coastal Seafood Gastronomy

  • Fried Pomfret & Tiger Prawns: Freshly caught sea fish marinated with turmeric, mustard, and green chili, fried to a crisp golden perfection on beachside stoves.
  • Crab Kalia & Lobster Masala: Rich and spicy Bengali style crab curries served with hot steamed rice and fresh lemon.
  • Green Coconut Waters: Naturally sweet and hydrating coastal coconuts sold in plenty along the Old Digha seawall.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is the distance between Digha and Mandarmani?

Mandarmani is approximately 25 km from Digha, accessible in 35 minutes via auto-rickshaw or taxi along the coastal link road.

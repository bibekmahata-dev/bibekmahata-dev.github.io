About Bengal Explorers

Bengal Explorers is an independent digital portal designed, coded, and curated by a passionate group of Class 12 students from Garhbeta and Paschim Medinipur, West Bengal.

🎯 Our Mission

We created this platform to bridge the gap in local travel technology for West Bengal. Travelers and everyday commuters often struggle to find unified live train schedules, state bus route connections, real-time weather, and authentic local travel recommendations in one clean place.

🛠️ What We Built

  • Live Transit Boards: Real-time departures for Howrah & Sealdah stations and inter-district bus routes.
  • Interactive Map: Leaflet GIS mapping with live multi-modal route calculations.
  • GPS Weather: Instant regional temperature, air quality, and sunrise tracking powered by Open-Meteo.
  • Cultural Calendar: 2026 festival dates and lunar phases for West Bengal holidays.

👥 The Team

Led by Bibek Mahata (Lead Developer) alongside a dedicated crew of student photographers, route researchers, and writers documenting our home state.

Privacy Policy & Terms

Last Updated: August 2026 • Bengal Explorers Community

1. Information We Collect

Bengal Explorers is a student-built public information portal. We do not collect personal identification data unless voluntarily provided. Location data used for live weather is processed client-side through your browser with permission and is never stored on external databases.

2. Cookies & Advertisements

We may use third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites on the Internet.

3. Transit & Weather Data

Train schedules, bus timetables, and weather forecasts are aggregated for informational purposes from open public sources and APIs. Commuters are advised to verify critical travel schedules with official station authorities.

4. Contact & Inquiries

For questions or feedback regarding this project, contact Bibek Mahata & the student team via email: bibek.workdev01@gmail.com or our official Instagram.

1. Travel & Trekking Safety Advisory

⚠️ Field Terrain Caution: Many of West Bengal's natural landscapes feature inherent physical risks. Visitors must exercise sound personal judgment and caution:

  • Gangani Grand Canyon (Garhbeta): The laterite clay gorges and cliff rims descend up to 70 feet. The fragile clay crumb can slip easily, especially during or after monsoon rainfall. Stay away from unbarricaded canyon crests and avoid venturing into gorge caves after sunset.
  • Darjeeling & High Altitude: High elevations (Tiger Hill at 8,482 ft) involve cold sub-zero winds and steep mountain roads. Shared jeep travel requires experienced Himalayan drivers. Dress in adequate thermal layers.
  • Sundarbans Mangrove Waters: Exploring the delta requires certified government-authorized boats and licensed forest guides. Never step onto muddy banks inside the tiger reserve core zone.
  • Purulia & Bankura Hills: Granite boulders around Ayodhya Hills and Bamni Falls can become extremely slippery during wet weather. Always wear proper grip hiking shoes.

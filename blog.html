Expedition to Gangani: The Grand Canyon of Bengal

A Geological Masterpiece Carved by the Sacred Silabati River in Garhbeta

Carved across millennia by the flowing waters of the Silabati River, Gangani plunges into 70-foot blood-red laterite gorges and natural earthen labyrinths. Revered in folklore as the mythical battlefield of Bhima and Bakasura, this geological wonder in Garhbeta is Bengal's ultimate landscape photography and canyon trekking destination.

Bishnupur: The Golden Terracotta Capital of the Malla Kings

Sundarbans: Into the Tidal Realm of the Royal Bengal Tiger

Navigating the World's Largest Halophytic Mangrove Delta & Estuarine Safaris
